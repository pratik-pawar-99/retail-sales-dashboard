# 📊 Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview

This is my **first Power BI dashboard project**, created as part of my learning journey in **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence**.

I used the **Online Retail dataset** to explore sales performance, customer revenue, country-wise sales, and product demand.

The goal of this project was to practice transforming raw data into meaningful business insights using interactive Power BI visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Identify the top countries by revenue
- Identify the top customers by revenue
- Understand product demand across different countries
- Practice creating business-focused dashboards in Power BI
- Improve my skills in data visualization and analysis

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard contains four main visualizations:

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend – 2011
A line chart showing how revenue changed month by month throughout 2011.

### 2. Top 10 Countries by Revenue
A combination chart showing the top 10 countries by revenue, excluding the UK, along with quantity sold.

### 3. Top 10 Customers by Revenue
A bar chart showing the customers who generated the highest revenue.

### 4. Product Demand by Country
A map visualization showing product demand across different countries, excluding the UK.
